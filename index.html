Experience Categories

Nepal refuses to be reduced to a single story. Whether you arrive for the peaks, the temples, or the silence — you'll leave with something unexpected.

🧗
Adventure
Trekking, climbing, white-water rafting, zip-lining, and bungee jumping over Himalayan gorges.
🏛️
Culture
UNESCO temples, living goddesses, Newari festivals, and ancient monasteries where monks chant at dawn.
🦏
Wildlife
One-horned rhinos, Bengal tigers, Asian elephants, and over 900 bird species across Chitwan and Bardia.
🕉️
Spirituality
Pashupatinath cremation ghats, Boudhanath chanting, Buddhist retreats, and high-altitude hermitages.

Dal bhat that fuels Himalayan expeditions, steamed momo dumplings, Newari feasts, and Ilam tea at altitude.
🏯
Heritage
Durbar Squares, bronze-age trade routes, living Newari architecture, and the birthplace of Siddhartha Gautama.

Beyond the Guidebook

Nepal's Hidden Gems

Places that demand real effort to reach — and reward it ten times over.

Entry № 1 · Karnali Province
Western Nepal · 2,990m

Rara Lake

Nepal's largest and deepest lake sits in a remote western valley almost no one visits. The 45-minute flight from Nepalgunj deposits you in a silence so complete you can hear the lake breathe. No roads. No mobile signal. Just an alpine mirror reflecting skies of impossible blue.

45 min Flight from Nepalgunj
10.8 km² Lake Surface
167m Max Depth
Entry № 2 · Sudurpashchim Province
Far Western Nepal · 3,300m

Khaptad National Park

An alpine plateau stretching 225 square kilometres, thick with rhododendrons and medicinal herbs. Khaptad Baba, a famous saint, meditated here for decades. The park sees fewer than 2,000 visitors per year — a number that feels like a privilege.

225 km² Protected Area
<2,000 Annual Visitors
270+ Bird Species
Entry № 3 · Palpa District
Western Hills · 1,371m

Tansen

A Newari merchant town preserved like amber. Its famous Dhamma Dhokha gate, narrow flagstone lanes, and brass-smiths still working century-old bellows make Tansen feel like Bhaktapur without the tourists. The sunset view of the Annapurna from Shreenagar Hill is one of Nepal's best-kept secrets.

1371m Elevation
Newari Architecture
4 hrs From Pokhara
Entry № 4 · Ilam District
Eastern Nepal · 1,600m

Ilam Tea Gardens

First-flush Ilam tea is considered one of South Asia's finest. The gardens stretch across mist-wrapped hills with Kanchenjunga as a backdrop. There are almost no other foreign visitors. You walk between rows of manicured bushes, share tea directly with pickers, and sleep to the sound of rain on tin roofs.

First-flush Tea Quality
1,600m Elevation
6 hrs From Biratnagar
